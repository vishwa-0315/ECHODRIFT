# ECHO DRIFT: Playtest kit

Test with **3–5 kids per age band** (10–12 on Cadet, 13–16 on Commander). Use one adult observer per child. Don't explain the game: the goal is to see whether it teaches itself.

## Script (about 30 minutes)

1. **Before playing (2 min, spoken, casual):**
   - "If you're floating in a space station, is there gravity up there?"
   - "If you looked at a star tonight, are you seeing it as it is right now?"
2. **Play World 1 (Cinder)** from the title screen. Say only: "Play however you like, and think out loud if you can."
3. **After World 1 (3 min):**
   - "How did you get the ship to land / reach the beacon?"
   - "Why did the moon help?"
   - Ask the two pre-questions again.
4. **Optional:** continue to Veil (World 2).

## What to watch for

| Moment | Write down |
|---|---|
| First flight | Seconds until the first successful drag. Did they need the "?" button? |
| Crashes | Did they laugh, or get frustrated? How many retries? Did they read the hint? |
| Investigate | Did they understand "pick a tool, then tap a place"? Did they ration charges? |
| Case Board | Did they pin evidence on purpose, or guess? Did anyone choose "Unknown"? |
| Colony | Did they notice the night power drop? Did they read the failure chain? |
| Report | Reaction to the 76-year wait (a good sign is "whoa" or a laugh) |
| Forge | Did they try iron? Did the Goldilocks zone moving away land emotionally? |

## Red flags to fix before release

- A child stuck for over 60 seconds with no idea what to do
- Any scary reaction to the Sun/red-giant reveal
- Text a 10-year-old can't read comfortably on their own phone
