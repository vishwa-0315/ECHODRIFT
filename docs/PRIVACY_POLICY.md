**Last updated:** [date]

ECHO DRIFT is made by [developer name] ("we"). It is designed for children aged 10–16 and their families and teachers.

**We collect no personal information.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the advertising ID and does not connect to the internet while you play.

**What is stored, and where:** game progress, settings and the learning report (stars, discoveries, time played) are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

**Links:** a few science facts link to sources such as NASA. These links sit behind a parental gate and open in the device's browser.
